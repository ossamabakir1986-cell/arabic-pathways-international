Arabic Pathways — International v0.2.2
Individual Sound Tiles

Purpose
-------
A0 learners should never need to press one button that sends a row of isolated Arabic letters to speech synthesis.

What changed
------------
1. Each isolated letter in the Learn step now has its own large audio tile.
2. The tile displays the letter and its Arabic letter name.
3. Tapping ب plays only باء; tapping ت plays only تاء, etc.
4. Vowelled syllables such as بَ / بِ / بُ are also individual audio tiles.
5. Single-letter examples use one compact audio button.
6. Normal and Slow controls remain for actual words and phrases, where speed comparison is useful.

This removes the string/blending problem by design. The browser voice may still sound robotic in local mode; the planned Gemini TTS connection addresses voice quality separately.
